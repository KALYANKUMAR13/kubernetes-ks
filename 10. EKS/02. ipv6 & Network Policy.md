**Network Policy**

Ingress and Egress traffic to the pod and from the pod.

IN Eks, these policy to pod are maintained by eBPF. this EBPF is neither a process nor a side car container, it is running in kernal where the linux is running, 


Whenever the pod is created, ebpf attaches the network policy as mentioned network policy.
